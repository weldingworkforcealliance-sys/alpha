# PCCC Fillet Weld Rubric — Prototype v1

This is a **PCCC instructional grading rubric aligned to the AWS SENSE workflow**. It is not presented as an AWS-issued percentage rubric.

## Five equal criteria

Each criterion is worth up to 20 raw points.

### 1. Consistency
Evaluates straightness, even width, even profile, and control from start to finish.

Quick instructor choices:
- 20 — Excellent
- 18 — Minor variation
- 16 — Noticeable variation
- 14 — Poor
- 10 — Major inconsistency

### 2. Weld Defects
Evaluates visible discontinuities and overall soundness.

Quick instructor choices:
- 20 — None
- 18 — Minor
- 16 — Several
- 14 — Major
- 10 — Severe

Quick visible-defect tags:
- Crack
- Lack of Fusion
- Undercut
- Porosity
- Overlap
- Slag Inclusion

### 3. Following Procedure
Evaluates setup, settings, electrode handling, angles, material preparation, cleaning, cooling, sequence, and safe work.

Quick instructor choices:
- 20 — Independent
- 18 — Minor help
- 16 — Some help
- 14 — Repeated help
- 10 — Major issue

### 4. Restarts
Evaluates crater treatment, tie-in, continuity, buildup, depression, and restart control.

Quick instructor choices:
- 20 — Seamless
- 18 — Good
- 16 — Noticeable
- 14 — Poor
- 10 — Major problem

### 5. Bead Size
Current rule for a 1/8-inch electrode:
- 3/16 in. minimum
- 1/4 in. target
- 3/8 in. maximum

Quick instructor choices:
- 20 — 1/4 in. target
- 18 — Near target
- 16 — Acceptable
- 14 — At limit
- 10 — Outside range

## Final-grade rule

`Final Grade = Raw Rubric Score × 0.95`

A raw 100 becomes 95%.

## Two-attempt rule

Each graded weld position and lab project has up to two attempts.

### Attempt 1
- Attempt 1 is the official grade until Attempt 2 is completed.
- Crack or lack of fusion causes an automatic F.
- The instructor still finishes the five criteria for diagnostic feedback.

### Attempt 2
- Attempt 2 is a true reassessment, not "best of two."
- Once complete, Attempt 2 replaces Attempt 1 even if the numeric result is lower.
- If Attempt 2 contains a crack or lack of fusion, Weld Defects = 0/20.
- Remaining criteria are scored normally.
- Maximum possible final grade with a critical defect on Attempt 2 is 76%.

## Instructor interaction rule

For a normal weld:
- five grading taps;
- no manual math;
- no Save button;
- auto-save after each tap;
- optional quick feedback tags;
- one-tap Next Student;
- Attempt 2 hidden until needed.
