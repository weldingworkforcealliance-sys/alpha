WLD 110 — Licensed Oxy-Fuel Cutting Courseware Guide

Day-specific OFC teaching support for the 23-day WLD 110 shop sequence.

Licensed-source rule: This LTG guide is a day-by-day teaching crosswalk derived from the school-purchased Chapter 8 — Oxygen Gas Cutting presentation. It is intended for the students/instructors covered by that purchase. The source presentation remains the controlling licensed courseware; PCCC procedures, manufacturer instructions and assigned welding procedures control actual shop operation.

Use this guide for

Equipment identification, regulators/hoses/tips, check valves and flashback protection, fuel-gas concepts, manual cutting, kerf/drag, operator-controlled variables, flashback/backfire and cylinder safety.

Day-by-day WLD 110 crosswalk

Day 1Equipment identification + pre-use inspection
Licensed courseware: slides 9–30
Identify torch, tips, regulators, hoses, cylinders, striker, securing equipment, check valves/flashback protection and PPE before operation.
Day 2External equipment checks
Licensed courseware: slides 24–30
Inspect regulators, hoses, fittings, torch components and reverse-flow/flashback protection; tag defects instead of improvising repairs.
Day 3Hazards + gas-cylinder controls
Licensed courseware: slides 87–93
Reinforce purge requirements, flashback/backfire awareness, oxygen cleanliness, upright/secured cylinders and separation of fuel-gas hazards.
Day 4OFC fundamentals + flame control
Licensed courseware: slides 43–61
Explain oxygen’s role, fuel-gas choices, acetylene characteristics, flame types and the requirement to follow manufacturer torch procedures.
Day 5Gas and fuel selection
Licensed courseware: slides 43–56
Compare oxygen and common fuel gases, then tie gas selection to the actual equipment and approved PCCC setup.
Day 6Straight-cut practice
Licensed courseware: slides 64–76
Use edge-start position, tip-to-work distance, steady travel, kerf and drag lines to coach the first controlled straight cuts.
Day 7Shutdown + abnormal events
Licensed courseware: slides 87–93
Review purge, flashback/backfire recognition, cylinder controls and the approved shutdown sequence before the checkpoint.
Day 8Integrated cutting practice
Licensed courseware: slides 64–76
Use constant tip distance, steady motion, kerf and drag as the visible evidence of control.
Day 9Workstation organization
Licensed courseware: slides 27–30, 87
Route hoses correctly, verify fittings and keep the torch/hoses purged and clear of traffic, sparks and hot metal.
Day 10Plate alignment + controlled cut
Licensed courseware: slides 64–76
Start from a known edge, maintain alignment and use kerf/drag evidence to judge travel control.
Day 11Cut-quality refinement
Licensed courseware: slides 72–86
Use kerf, drag, tip size, gas flow/pressure, standoff and cutting speed as distinct variables when diagnosing cut quality.
Day 12OFC performance checkpoint
Licensed courseware: slides 79–93
Evaluate operator-controlled variables plus purge, flashback/backfire response and safe gas handling.
Day 13Full supervised OFC operation
Licensed courseware: slides 57–68
Follow the manufacturer-controlled lighting/operation sequence and use steady manual cutting technique from start through travel.
Day 14Project-cut support
Licensed courseware: slides 72–86
Tie approved project dimensions to kerf control, tip size, gas flow/pressure, standoff and speed.
Day 15OFC troubleshooting
Licensed courseware: slides 79–90
Use the variable list, speed effects and flashback/backfire causes to isolate the problem before changing settings.
Day 16Performance + project completion
Licensed courseware: slides 72–86
Use kerf/drag and controlled variables as the quality evidence for final project cuts.
Day 17Repeated straight/square cuts
Licensed courseware: slides 64–86
Repeat edge starts, constant tip distance and controlled speed; compare cut faces as a batch instead of one part at a time.
Day 18Project cutting
Licensed courseware: slides 64–86
Maintain consistent edge-start technique, kerf allowance, standoff and travel during repeated project parts.
Day 19Cut correction
Licensed courseware: slides 79–90
Correct only the variable supported by the evidence: tip, pressure/flow, speed, standoff, cleanliness or abnormal torch event.
Day 20Final OFC evidence
Licensed courseware: slides 81–93
Close performance evidence with operator variables, hose/torch purge, flashback/backfire response and cylinder safety.
Day 21Steel Dice cutting support
Licensed courseware: slides 64–86
Use approved cut geometry with controlled edge starts, kerf allowance, tip distance and travel speed.
Day 22Steel Dice fit-up support
Licensed courseware: slides 72–86
Use kerf, drag and cut-face evidence to decide whether a face is ready for fit-up or requires correction.
Day 23Final OFC evidence + closeout
Licensed courseware: slides 57–93
Review operation, manual cutting, kerf/drag, variables, purge, flashback/backfire and safe gas handling before final closeout.
