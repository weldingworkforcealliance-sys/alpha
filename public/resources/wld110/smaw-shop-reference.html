WLD 110 — SMAW Shop Reference

Daily shop reference for E6010/E6011 and E7018 practice in WLD 110. The assigned WPS/SWPS, instructor direction, machine capability, and approved PCCC procedure control actual welding variables.

Use the procedure first. Electrode classification, polarity/current, position, joint, coupon, and acceptance requirements must match the assigned work. Never “fix” a weld by changing unapproved variables.

CLAMS quick check

C — Current
Use the approved procedure/range and machine setup. Do not substitute a remembered setting for the assigned procedure.
L — Length of arc
Keep a controlled arc. Excessive arc length commonly destabilizes the puddle and increases spatter/defects.
A — Angle
Set work angle and travel angle for the joint/position, then keep them consistent through the weld.
M/S — Manipulation & Speed
Control puddle placement and travel. Avoid weaving or travel changes that are not part of the demonstrated procedure.

Electrode reminders

E6010 / E6011All-position electrodes commonly used for strong digging arc characteristics and early skill development. Follow the assigned polarity/current and procedure.
E7018Low-hydrogen electrode commonly used for structural work. Protect electrode condition/storage as required and use the assigned procedure.
InspectionCheck placement, straightness, tie-in, undercut/overlap, porosity, slag/cleaning, starts/stops, consistency, and weld size/profile before deciding what to correct.

Day-by-day WLD 110 focus

Day 1Helmet fit, leads/work connection, machine area, E6010/E6011 arc starts, arc length, body position, and controlled stopping.
Day 2Flat E6010/E6011 consistency: repeat starts, travel, arc length, work angle, and bead placement.
Day 3Introduce 1F fillet control: joint orientation, electrode angle, puddle location, tie-in, and cleaning between attempts.
Day 4Corrective 1F sets: identify one defect, connect it to technique, change one variable, and compare the next weld.
Day 5E7018 introduction: verify approved storage/condition, polarity/procedure, tight arc, and slag control.
Day 6E7018 1F fillets: bead placement, travel speed, work/travel angle, tie-in, profile, and complete slag removal.
Day 71F performance checkpoint: safe setup plus repeatable starts, placement, profile, and cleanup.
Day 8Integrated 1F practice: apply print/layout requirements to coupon preparation and weld location.
Day 9Transition to 2F with E6010/E6011: stable stance, horizontal fillet orientation, toe placement, and puddle support.
Day 10E7018 2F: tight arc, controlled travel, profile, toe tie-in, and slag removal.
Day 112F refinement: use visual inspection to distinguish placement, travel, arc-length, and fit-up problems.
Day 122F performance checkpoint: repeatable welds, inspection, correction decision, and evidence recording.
Day 13Begin 3F vertical-up E6010/E6011: stable body position, puddle support, sidewall tie-in, and deliberate progression.
Day 14E7018 3F: tight arc, controlled manipulation, sidewall tie-in, heat control, and slag management.
Day 153F troubleshooting: inspect undercut, overlap, inconsistent width, slag, tie-in, and starts/stops before retrying.
Day 163F performance checkpoint: demonstrate repeatability and document correction/recheck evidence.
Day 173F production practice while Steel Dice layout begins in WLD 105: maintain coupon discipline and do not let project work replace required weld practice.
Day 183F production: repeat technique under normal shop interruptions while preserving inspection and cleanup quality.
Day 193F correction/assembly support: resolve fit-up and weld-quality issues before adding more heat.
Day 20Final 3F evidence and transition planning: complete required checks before moving to overhead work.
Day 21Begin 4F E6010/E6011: overhead PPE coverage, stable work height, controlled puddle, travel, and falling-slag awareness.
Day 22E7018 4F: tight arc, toe control, profile, slag removal, and stable overhead body position.
Day 23Final SMAW evidence: finish remaining 4F checks, written/performance evidence, record completion, and professional shop closeout.
