WLD 110 — Licensed SMAW Courseware Guide

Day-specific SMAW teaching support for the 23-day WLD 110 shop sequence.

Licensed-source rule: This LTG guide is a day-by-day teaching crosswalk derived from the school-purchased Chapter 4 — Shielded Metal Arc Welding presentation. It is intended for the students/instructors covered by that purchase. The source presentation remains the controlling licensed courseware; PCCC procedures, manufacturer instructions and assigned welding procedures control actual shop operation.

Use this guide for

Equipment and circuit checks, electrode classification and storage, E6010/E6011/E7018 differences, welding variables, position/orientation, arc starting, arc blow, crater control and performance review.

Day-by-day WLD 110 crosswalk

Day 1Equipment inspection + first arc starts
Licensed courseware: slides 17–32, 63–65
Identify the power source, leads, holder and work connection. Demonstrate tap/scratch arc starts and safe correction of a stuck electrode.
Day 2External equipment checks + 1F consistency
Licensed courseware: slides 26–32, 57–62
Inspect cable condition, holder jaws and work connection. Coach one variable at a time: angle, arc length, travel speed and bead placement.
Day 31F fillet introduction
Licensed courseware: slides 57–62
Use work angle/travel angle language before welding. Compare bead shape to travel speed and arc length instead of guessing.
Day 41F corrective practice
Licensed courseware: slides 60–62, 67
Use arc length, travel speed and visible bead condition to diagnose the next correction; introduce arc blow only when the symptoms fit.
Day 5E7018 introduction
Licensed courseware: slides 39–51
Decode electrode classification, compare E6010/E6011/E7018 behavior, and emphasize low-hydrogen storage/conditioning for E7018.
Day 6E7018 1F fillets
Licensed courseware: slides 50–51, 57–62
Connect E7018 handling to electrode orientation, arc length, travel speed, tie-in and slag removal.
Day 71F performance checkpoint
Licensed courseware: slides 63–70
Require complete setup, controlled arc start, consistent travel and a deliberate crater/arc-ending technique.
Day 8Integrated 1F practice
Licensed courseware: slides 57–70
Use work angle, travel angle, arc length, speed, start/stop and crater control as the inspection language for the day.
Day 92F transition with E6010/E6011
Licensed courseware: slides 43–45, 57–59
Review cellulosic electrode behavior and set the 2F work/travel angles before students begin repetitions.
Day 10E7018 2F
Licensed courseware: slides 50–51, 57–62
Reinforce low-hydrogen handling, 2F orientation, tight arc control, travel speed and slag removal.
Day 112F refinement
Licensed courseware: slides 60–62, 67
Diagnose bead placement, arc length, speed and possible arc blow from the weld evidence before changing technique.
Day 122F performance checkpoint
Licensed courseware: slides 57–70
Use the full operation sequence: setup, arc start, travel/orientation, visual inspection and correct arc termination.
Day 133F E6010/E6011
Licensed courseware: slides 43–45, 54, 57–59
Connect cellulosic electrode characteristics to vertical-up positioning and the 3F orientation shown in the courseware.
Day 14E7018 3F
Licensed courseware: slides 48–51, 54, 57–59
Reinforce low-hydrogen control and vertical-up E7018 orientation before production practice.
Day 153F troubleshooting
Licensed courseware: slides 60–70
Inspect arc length, travel speed, bead shape, starts/stops, crater condition and arc blow as separate possible causes.
Day 163F performance checkpoint
Licensed courseware: slides 57–70
Require repeatable position/orientation and controlled start, travel and finish before recording performance evidence.
Day 173F production practice
Licensed courseware: slides 57–62
Keep technique stable during production: work angle, travel angle, arc length and speed stay observable and coachable.
Day 183F production + project work
Licensed courseware: slides 57–62
Use the courseware variables as the common language when comparing repeated welds across students.
Day 19Correction + assembly support
Licensed courseware: slides 60–70
Correct visible problems before adding heat: travel, arc length, crater fill and arc-blow symptoms should be isolated.
Day 20Final 3F evidence
Licensed courseware: slides 57–70
Use the complete SMAW operation and weld-variable review to close missing evidence before moving to overhead.
Day 214F E6010/E6011
Licensed courseware: slides 43–45, 54, 57–59
Review cellulosic electrode behavior and the 4F orientation/overhead setup before the first overhead repetitions.
Day 224F E7018
Licensed courseware: slides 48–51, 54, 57–59
Connect E7018 condition/storage to 4F orientation, controlled arc length and stable overhead body position.
Day 23Final SMAW evidence
Licensed courseware: slides 39–70
Use classification, equipment, variables, operation, starts/stops, arc blow and crater control as the final review domains.
