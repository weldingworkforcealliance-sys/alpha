WLD 110 — Licensed Welding Safety Courseware Guide

Daily safety reinforcement matched to the 23-day WLD 110 shop sequence.

Licensed-source rule: This LTG guide is a day-by-day teaching crosswalk derived from the school-purchased Chapter 2 — Welding Safety presentation. It is intended for the students/instructors covered by that purchase. The source presentation remains the controlling licensed courseware; PCCC procedures, manufacturer instructions and assigned welding procedures control actual shop operation.

Use this guide for

Stop-work expectations, burns/fire prevention, radiation, fumes/ventilation, electric shock, compressed gases, shop organization, PPE, hearing/respiratory protection and final safe closeout.

Day-by-day WLD 110 crosswalk

Day 1Stop-work authority, PPE + shop orientation
Licensed courseware: slides 4–6, 33–46
Students should know they can stop when something is unsafe, identify required PPE, protect eyes/skin and keep the shop/walkways controlled.
Day 2Electrical equipment + safe handling
Licensed courseware: slides 22–23, 33–44
Treat electrode/work circuits as energized hazards, inspect equipment and maintain appropriate eye, clothing, glove and footwear protection.
Day 3Fire, radiation + hot-metal zones
Licensed courseware: slides 11–16
Control combustibles, screens, fire protection and arc radiation before 1F work begins.
Day 4Compressed-gas cylinder safety
Licensed courseware: slides 25–32
Secure cylinders, keep oxygen equipment free of oil/grease, respect acetylene limits and protect cylinders from impact/heat.
Day 5Fumes, ventilation + PPE
Licensed courseware: slides 17–20, 35–50
Keep the breathing zone controlled, use ventilation appropriate to the operation and verify eye/face/body protection.
Day 6Cylinder + torch-area safety
Licensed courseware: slides 25–32, 40–49
Recheck cylinder security, oxygen cleanliness, eye protection, gloves/boots and the clear cutting lane before flame-on work.
Day 7Shutdown, fire prevention + gas safety
Licensed courseware: slides 11–13, 25–32
Treat shutdown as a safety task: no unattended hot work, cylinders secured/closed as required and fire hazards controlled.
Day 8PPE + organized shop
Licensed courseware: slides 33–49
Use screens, clear walkways, safety glasses under the helmet, intact clothing, gloves and appropriate footwear.
Day 9Housekeeping + trip hazards
Licensed courseware: slides 33–34, 42–49
Keep leads/hoses and walkways controlled, protect others with screens and maintain intact clothing/gloves/footwear.
Day 10Electrical + hand/body positioning
Licensed courseware: slides 22–23, 40–46
Keep the operator insulated and dry, use intact PPE and secure work before cutting or welding.
Day 11Fire prevention during cutting/welding
Licensed courseware: slides 11–13
Remove or protect combustibles, maintain extinguishing capability and use hot-work controls when outside a designated area.
Day 12PPE + respiratory readiness
Licensed courseware: slides 35–56
Verify helmet/shade, safety glasses, clothing, gloves/boots, hearing and ventilation/respiratory controls before performance work.
Day 13Compressed gas + flame-on work
Licensed courseware: slides 25–32
Reinforce cylinder security, oxygen cleanliness, approved lighting devices and separation from sparks/hot work.
Day 14Project hot-work controls
Licensed courseware: slides 11–13, 33–49
Control sparks, hot metal, screens, PPE and fire exposure as project cutting begins.
Day 15Troubleshooting without creating hazards
Licensed courseware: slides 17–23, 25–32
Make equipment safe before troubleshooting; maintain ventilation and electrical/gas-cylinder controls while diagnosing.
Day 16PPE during grinding, rework + hot metal
Licensed courseware: slides 35–51
Match PPE to welding, cutting and grinding; protect eyes, hands, feet, hearing and exposed skin during rework.
Day 17Production housekeeping + hot-metal identification
Licensed courseware: slides 11–13, 33–49
Maintain clean walkways, screens, fire controls, PPE and explicit hot-metal identification during repeated operations.
Day 18Cutting/drilling/grinding zones
Licensed courseware: slides 11–13, 33–51
Separate active work zones and protect personnel from sparks, radiation, noise and hot material.
Day 19Assembly correction + fire controls
Licensed courseware: slides 11–13, 33–51
Do not let rework bypass fire prevention, PPE, screens, hearing protection or hot-metal controls.
Day 20Evaluation without shortcuts
Licensed courseware: slides 4, 11–13, 35–51
Performance evaluation does not reduce stop-work authority, PPE, fire prevention, ventilation or cleanup requirements.
Day 21Overhead welding protection
Licensed courseware: slides 14–16, 35–51
Protect against radiation plus falling sparks/slag with correct helmet, intact clothing, gloves, footwear and added protective garments as needed.
Day 22Overhead E7018 + fixture safety
Licensed courseware: slides 14–16, 35–51
Reconfirm overhead PPE coverage, screens, stable work access and protection from falling sparks/slag.
Day 23Final shop closeout
Licensed courseware: slides 4, 11–13, 25–33, 35–53
Close with safe shutdown, cylinder/equipment accountability, hot-metal identification, PPE, housekeeping and hazard communication/SDS awareness.
