WLD 105 Welding Math

Scale Repeated Fractional Parts

Goal

Multiply a fraction or mixed-number part length by a whole-number quantity, simplify the result, and record the net stock required.

Book connection: Unit 9 — Multiplication of Common Fractions

Visual Model

Rule

Fraction × quantity

8 × 7/16 = 56/16 = 3 1/2 in

Multiply the numerator by the whole-number quantity. Keep the denominator, then simplify.

Mixed number × quantity

4 × 2 5/8 = 4 × 21/8 = 84/8 = 10 1/2 in

Convert the mixed number to an improper fraction before multiplying.

Before Live Classroom

  1. Double 3/8 in and explain why the denominator does not double.
  2. Find the net length for 8 tabs at 7/16 in each.
  3. Write quantity × part length = net stock required for one cut-list line.

Instructor note: If students finish quickly, convert the final total from inches to feet and inches. Do not add or multiply denominators when the operation does not require it.
