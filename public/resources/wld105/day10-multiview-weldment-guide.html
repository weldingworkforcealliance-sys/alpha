WLD 105 — Day 10 Multi-View Weldment Print

40-minute blueprint focus: Read the print as one fabrication document: views, dimensions, layout relationships, notes, tolerance, and weld information.

Controlling-source rule: This training print supports classroom practice. The school-authorized AWS A2.4 reference, assigned text, WPS/SWPS, and instructor-issued production print control when a conflict exists.

1. Identify the job

  • Read title block and units.
  • Confirm material.
  • Identify base and vertical plate.
  • Locate general tolerance.

2. Coordinate the views

  • Front: width and plate height.
  • Top: depth, holes, plate position.
  • Right side: centered plate location.
  • Use aligned dimensions, not appearance.

3. Lay out the parts

  • Use 1.00–6.00–1.00 chains.
  • Use hole center dimensions.
  • Use 2.50 centerline dimensions for plate centering.
  • Measure → calculate → check → record.

4. Read fabrication requirements

  • 2X Ø .375 THRU.
  • 1/4 fillet, both sides, 6.00 continuous.
  • Deburr edges and holes.
  • Verify size, location, centering, fit-up.

Student Evidence Check

Point to the exact print evidence for each answer.

  • What gets made, from what material, and to what finished size?
  • Where are both hole centers located from the ends and from the front edge?
  • How is the vertical plate centered on the 5.00 base depth?
  • What weld size, side condition, and length are specified?
  • Which dimensions carry a specific tolerance, and what general tolerance applies elsewhere?
  • What must be verified before welding?
Instructor Support — 40-Minute Flow

0–7 min

Retrieval: title block, material, three views, tolerance, weld symbol. Require pointing, not guessing.

7–16 min

Model one complete read-through. Show how dimensions transfer across aligned views.

16–24 min

Students identify finished sizes, holes, welds, and layout chains directly from the print.

24–33 min

Connect dimensions to layout, fit-up, tolerance, and inspection. Reject unsupported assumptions.

33–37 min

Reinforce MEASURE → CALCULATE → CHECK → RECORD using one hole or centering decision.

37–40 min

Launch the connected blueprint check and keep this print open as the classroom reference.
