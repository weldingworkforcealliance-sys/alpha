WLD 105 — Welding Math Lesson Card

Build Fraction Sense • 20 minutes

Goal

Name, compare, and simplify common fractions used in inch measurement.

Book connection: Unit 5 — Introduction to Common Fractions

Lesson Sequence

TimeActivity
0–2 minStart: Point to 3/8 on a tape. Identify the numerator and denominator and explain what each means.
2–6 minShow: Demonstrate 12/16 = 6/8 = 3/4. Compare 5/8 and 1/2 using a common denominator.
6–12 minStudents practice: Simplify 8/12 and 10/16. Order 1/4, 3/8, 1/2 from least to greatest.
12–18 minUse it: Find two equivalent labels for one measurement on a tape or marked inch and explain why both names represent the same location.
18–20 minQuick check: Simplify 18/24.

Instructor Only

Notes: Tie each fraction to equal parts of one whole. If needed, use a marked inch. Extend strong students to thirty-seconds.

Answers / checks: Model: 5/8 > 1/2. Guided: 8/12 = 2/3; 10/16 = 5/8; 1/4 < 3/8 < 1/2. Exit: 18/24 = 3/4.
