Student Workflow

Before writing the cut list

  1. Read title/notes and material.
  2. Identify quantity.
  3. Read all controlling dimensions.
  4. Identify holes, angles, edge prep, or other features.
  5. Check units and tolerances.

Before approving the cut list

  1. Trace every line back to the print.
  2. Verify quantity and material.
  3. Verify dimensions and features.
  4. Confirm units.
  5. Mark the QC checkpoint before cutting.
