WLD 114 · Torchmate operator hub

The full 5:00–6:00 PM WLD 114 class follows Lincoln Electric Learning · Torchmate 4000 learning plan videos on the Torchmate 4800 with FlexCut 80. The instructor alone accesses and plays the videos; students watch together and follow along in CAD or at the machine. Complete the nameplate on Days 5–7, welcome sign on Days 7–9 and capstone on Days 9–15; all seven Academy courses and the three projects are due by Day 15 · December 7. Approved cutting slots may rotate into the paired 6:00–9:45 PM WLD 115 lab while classmates continue welding, subject to direct instructor supervision and separate course evidence. Verify the installed torch, consumables and approved cut chart for the material and thickness before cutting; 80 A identifies the power-source rating, not a universal cutting setting. The Academy links on this page are for the signed-in instructor; students do not need Academy accounts.

Instructor release gate: Students do not energize, move, or cut until the instructor checks the equipment, file, toolpath, plate, air supply, consumables, grounding, cut envelope, and people clear of the table. Stop and isolate a machine with an unexpected motion, electrical, air, or torch fault. Service work belongs to authorized personnel.

Daily workflow

  1. Before startup: identify the exact table and plasma source; check approved PPE, extraction, machine area, cable routing, dry air, consumables, plate stability, work return and visible damage.
  2. Prepare: name and save the file; check dimensions, kerf offset, cut order, leads, small features, pierce locations and stock margin. Preview the path and have the instructor approve it.
  3. First motion: follow the machine-specific VMD SP2 startup and reference procedure. Dry-run/trace the job where the approved machine setup permits it. Confirm travel and plate alignment before any live cut.
  4. Cut and inspect: supervise the run, stay at the controls, stop if motion or arc behavior is abnormal; let hot material cool before handling. Label the sample and log material, consumable, settings, dimensions, defect and one corrective action.
  5. Closeout: follow the machine's shutdown procedure, remove scrap safely, clean the work area, inspect consumables and leave a machine status note. Use only the lubrication procedure for the exact installed machine.

WLD 115 cutting rotation: Assign each learner a CNC slot and a separate welding task. The instructor calls the learner to the table, checks the WLD 114 cut gate, supervises the cut and records WLD 114 evidence; classmates keep welding under adequate shop supervision. Stop the rotation whenever supervision, machine readiness or welding instruction is compromised. The next learner does not approach until the prior cut is safe and the station is ready.

Maintenance boundary

Daily student closeout means visual inspection, scrap and debris control, consumable check and a clean station. The 4000 Series user guide describes cleaning the breakaway assembly and a separate lubrication method for rail cassettes; it does not establish a universal daily WD-40 rail-spray instruction. The WLD 114 source planner's WD-40 instruction is therefore held for model-specific review. Do not spray the AVHC or substitute a lubricant without the approved manual. 4000 Series maintenance reference
