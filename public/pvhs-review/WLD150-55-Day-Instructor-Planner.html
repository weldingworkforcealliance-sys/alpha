Source and adaptation decisions

Course designation, title, outcomes and 4-credit field identify this as WLD 150 Advanced Processes. The source description says WLD-120 and the co-requisite line repeats WLD-150; this edition uses WLD 150 and the WLD 120 pairing supported by both syllabi scheduling notes, and flags the source errors for institutional correction. The guide leaves minutes local; this edition uses 120 minutes. No online AWS coursework is assigned. Source reference to an original 24-day model does not verify the official night calendar.

Credits in the supplied syllabus: 4. Credit/contact-hour arithmetic has not been independently approved by the registrar.

For retries, retain every attempt, its evidence, rubric version and feedback. Weekly conduct reviews require actual attendance and dated conduct evidence. Do not infer conduct grades from lesson completion. A syllabus statement that 75% of students should earn 70% is a cohort success target, not an individual automatic-passing rule.

Bend-test pass/fail uses the instructor-selected procedure and named acceptance criteria. The academic rubric evaluates documented performance and analysis separately. A completed class test does not establish AWS or other certification.

Capstone tasks that cannot be supervised within available time remain held or are resequenced with a recorded deviation.
