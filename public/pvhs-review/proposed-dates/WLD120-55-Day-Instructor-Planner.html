Outcomes: CLO 1, CLO 2

Daily objective

Safety and equipment

Check required PPE, ventilation, fire controls, screens, leads and work area before work. Identify hot material and stop unsafe work immediately. Students report defects; repairs and energy isolation are performed only by authorized personnel under the applicable procedure. No unattended machine, live arc or destructive test. Teaching sequence

  • Set course standards for PPE, communication, cleanup, tool control, documentation, and how approvals must happen before material is committed.
  • Introduce the capstone model: each student or team owns a component that must fit a larger class assembly, so individual mistakes affect the full build.
  • Walk the class through the production path from print review to final presentation so students see where their work is heading.
  • Use prior projects, photos, or sample parts to show the difference between a finished fabrication workflow and a pile of random steel.

Teaching guidance

Do not let this become a vague pep talk. Show the path, name the gates, and make students repeat the sequence back to you.

Coaching

Tone-setting day. Capstone gets easier when the workflow is predictable from the start.
