WLD 114

The supplied WLD-114 committee syllabus controls course identity and lists 2 credits. WLD-CNC-P is mapped to WLD 114 because the supplied title, Torchmate content and WLD-115 pairing match; this is a documented content mapping, not a registrar change. The legacy 08:00 start is replaced by the requested PVHS section windows. Maintenance product instructions are held for installed-manual verification. Academy title/access verification and the separately named AWS safety certificate remain prerequisites to claiming completion.

WLD 115

The supplied 55-day source uses 190 minutes. This edition retains all 55 day topics and the GMAW/FCAW/GTAW sequence while using 120 minutes per user direction. Reduce repetitions to fit the block; do not skip setup, inspection or safe closeout. The original six-week syllabus still controls outcomes and weights. Ferrous and non-ferrous GTAW evidence is required by the syllabus, even where the guide says introduce as appropriate.

WLD 120

The supplied 55-day capstone guide confirms 60-minute meetings and QC gates on Days 20, 30 and 40. This edition preserves those gates. The six-week syllabus does not establish an official 23- or 24-meeting calendar. All PVHS dates remain unset.

WLD 150

Course designation, title, outcomes and 4-credit field identify this as WLD 150 Advanced Processes. The source description says WLD-120 and the co-requisite line repeats WLD-150; this edition uses WLD 150 and the WLD 120 pairing supported by both syllabi scheduling notes, and flags the source errors for institutional correction. The guide leaves minutes local; this edition uses 120 minutes. No online AWS coursework is assigned. Source reference to an original 24-day model does not verify the official night calendar.
