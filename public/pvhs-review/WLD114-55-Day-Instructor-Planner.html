Paired-course capacity

In 114/115, instructor-prepared coupons may be needed before students reach CNC CAD production. In 120/150, bend-test work on Days 26–34 and pipe work afterward retain priority in the advanced-process block. Capstone tasks that cannot be supervised within available time remain held or are resequenced with a recorded deviation.

Day 1
Safety First (complete in one day) + Lab Standards

Outcomes: CLO 1

Daily objective

By the end of this meeting, produce and explain safety response and supervised inspection record: baseline and stated acceptance target. Demonstrate the assigned safety and readiness checks and explain when to stop; instructor observation is required before any machine authorization.

Instructor preparation

Display the drawing/procedure and acceptance criteria for safety first (complete in one day) + lab standards. Stage the listed materials and a relevant example. Review each student’s prior attempt and outstanding hold.

Safety and equipment

Check required PPE, ventilation, fire controls, screens, leads and work area before work. Identify hot material and stop unsafe work immediately. Students report defects; repairs and energy isolation are performed only by authorized personnel under the applicable procedure. No unattended machine, live arc or destructive test. Verify the installed Torchmate 4800/FlexCut 80 configuration, approved consumables, material and cut chart. Instructor releases file, path, alignment, settings and clear work envelope before motion or firing. Follow the installed equipment manual for maintenance and shutdown; the source WD-40 instruction is not adopted without manufacturer confirmation. Torchmate 4800 and FlexCut 80; installed-controller manual and cut chart; instructor-authorized Academy access; CAD workstation and approved file storage; sample consumables; approved stock; suitable measuring tools; tagged sample cuts; daily inspection sheet.

Teaching sequence

  • Torchmate Academy: Safety First module + quiz completion
  • Lab walkthrough: gantry, rails, slats, air system, consumables, emergency stop
  • Teach the ‘no surprises’ rule: announce motion, hands clear, eyes up
  • Introduce file hygiene: naming rules + where files live

Coaching

Make Day 1 count. Students should leave knowing exactly what ‘safe CNC’ looks like.
