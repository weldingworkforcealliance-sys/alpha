Counting rules and unresolved time

Source: supplied Passaic Valley Regional High School Academic Calendar 2026-2027, approved February 24, 2026. Count weekdays when students attend; exclude weekends, school closures and the March 9 staff-only day. Each pair meets together, so 55 dates serve both courses, not 110 dates for each pair. The second pair begins on the next student day.

Early-dismissal and exam days count provisionally as student days, but the PDF does not provide the available welding blocks. Seven flagged dates occur in the first pair and six in the second. Confirm B and C attendance, travel, exam obligations and available minutes before activation. Do not automatically shorten or mark complete a 60- or 120-minute lesson.

The full window has 112 scheduled student days. The proposed pairs consume 110, leaving June 17 and 18, both exam/early-dismissal days. If all early-dismissal/exam dates are unavailable, only 97 candidate days remain through June 18: 13 fewer than needed. No additional dates after the school year are inferred. The source says emergency closings beyond three days will be made up where applicable; their dates are not specified.

Normal proposed windows remain B 08:30-09:30 / 09:30-11:30 and C 11:30-12:30 / 12:30-14:30. WLD114/120 require 60 minutes; WLD115/150 require 120 minutes. A nominal 55-day allocation is 55 hours for each 60-minute course and 110 hours for each 120-minute course; this draft does not certify actual delivered contact hours.

Closures excluded from this draft

December 24-January 1; January 18; February 15-16; March 9; March 26-April 2; May 31. Weekends are also excluded.

Proposed checkpoints

WLD120 Day20: April27; Day30: May11; Day40: May25. WLD150 Day31 bend-test review: May12. These dates move with any approved calendar change; technical releases remain per student and evidence-based.
