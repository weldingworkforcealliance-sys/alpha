Source and adaptation decisions

The supplied 55-day source uses 190 minutes. This edition retains all 55 day topics and the GMAW/FCAW/GTAW sequence while using 120 minutes per user direction. Reduce repetitions to fit the block; do not skip setup, inspection or safe closeout. The original six-week syllabus still controls outcomes and weights. Ferrous and non-ferrous GTAW evidence is required by the syllabus, even where the guide says introduce as appropriate.

Credits in the supplied syllabus: 4. Credit/contact-hour arithmetic has not been independently approved by the registrar.

These project-specific values must come from the authorized documents, not from a generic rubric. Keep completed student forms and notes in the correct B or C section. Capstone tasks that cannot be supervised within available time remain held or are resequenced with a recorded deviation.

WLD 115 • WEEK 1 – GMAW (MIG) – Foundations + setup habits • 120 minutes

Day 1
Skill focus + baseline coupons

Outcomes: CLO 1, CLO 2, CLO 3, CLO 6

Daily objective

By the end of this meeting, produce and explain gMAW setup explanation and labeled stringer-bead coupons: baseline and stated acceptance target. The assigned process and position are correctly identified; actual setup and visible weld features are compared with the instructor-provided task criteria; one correction is demonstrated and logged.

Instructor preparation

Display the drawing/procedure and acceptance criteria for skill focus + baseline coupons. Stage the listed materials and a relevant example. Review each student’s prior attempt and outstanding hold.

Safety and equipment

Check required PPE, ventilation, fire controls, screens, leads and work area before work. Identify hot material and stop unsafe work immediately. Students report defects; repairs and energy isolation are performed only by authorized personnel under the applicable procedure. No unattended machine, live arc or destructive test. Verify process, polarity, wire or filler, shielding requirements and approved procedure before energizing. Confirm FCAW wire type before deciding whether external shielding is required. Use process-appropriate torch, tungsten preparation, gas handling and hot-metal controls. Position progression requires instructor readiness review. Assigned GMAW/FCAW/GTAW power source and compatible gun or torch; approved wire/filler, gas where applicable and regulators; prepared coupons; clamps and fixtures; appropriate cleaning tools; weld gauges and measuring tools; approved process procedure and parameter log.

Teaching sequence

  • Safety inspection and defect reporting; authorized personnel handle repairs
  • Wire, gas, and settings verification (students explain setup before welding)
  • Repetition sets: stringer beads and controlled travel (consistency before speed)
  • Fillet weld progression with parameter adjustments (quality > quantity)
  • End-of-day: label coupons + log settings that worked

Coaching

Keep goals measurable. One correction at a time. Logging is not optional.
