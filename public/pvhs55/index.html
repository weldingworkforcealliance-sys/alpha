Daily instructor workflow

  1. Select the correct cohort and course section in LTG. Each cohort has separate enrollment, attendance, grades and notes.
  2. Open the scheduled lesson. Check objectives, equipment, safety, daily timing and required evidence before instruction.
  3. Verify student enrollment and individual authorization before machine work. Record CNC authorization for the exact machine and operation.
  4. For WLD 120, record QC reviews at Day 20 (April 27), Day 30 (May 11) and Day 40 (May 25), using the same job and drawing revision. A hold or revocation requires downstream work to be reviewed again.
  5. For WLD 150, retain every bend-test attempt and record the observed result at Day 31 (May 12). A failed or invalid result cannot release the checkpoint.
  6. Grade only observed work with supporting evidence and a rubric. Leave pending assessments unscored. Record lesson notes and actual delivery separately from student grades or signoffs.
