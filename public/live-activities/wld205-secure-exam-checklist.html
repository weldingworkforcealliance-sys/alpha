WLD 205 — Secure AWS SENSE II Exam Administration Checklist

Use for Day 22 Trade Math and Day 31 Welding Metallurgy. The authorized AWS testing system/procedure controls if any instruction differs.

Before Students Enter

  • Verify approved roster and identity workflow.
  • Confirm approved accommodations and how they will be delivered without exposing secure content.
  • Verify devices/materials/access and testing environment.
  • Remove unauthorized notes, copied questions, screenshots, practice keys, or unsecured reference materials.
  • Confirm the authorized method for recording completion/pass/retest/retrain status.

During Testing

  • Follow the approved exam timing and administration procedure.
  • Do not coach, paraphrase, define, summarize, or reconstruct secure questions.
  • Document only administrative incidents permitted by the testing procedure.
  • If a technical issue occurs, pause/resolve it through the authorized procedure rather than recreating an item elsewhere.

Closeout

  • Collect/secure all authorized testing materials.
  • Close or sign out of the secure testing environment as required.
  • Record only permitted status/evidence in LTG.
  • For remediation, record the broad approved domain/status only. Do not copy or describe secure questions.
LTG secure-content boundary: Never store secure questions, answer choices, correct answers, screenshots, copied prompts, reconstructed questions, or unauthorized acceptance content.
