WLD 205 Instructor Support — Days 10–33

Use this guide with the authorized print, WPS/SWPS, material/metallurgy references, licensed Chasan pages, and actual WLD-210 evidence named in the planner. It supplements the planner. It does not replace a governing source.

Standing workflow: SOURCE → CALCULATE / INTERPRET → PLAN → VERIFY → RECORD

Day 10 — Overlap Day

Instructor move: Incoming PCCC students complete only the connected 20-minute math diagnostic. PVHS stays in WLD-210. After the diagnostic, incoming PCCC joins WLD-210.

Do not launch: common WLD-205 theory, blueprint packet, metallurgy lesson, or formal Level II math lesson.

Evidence: diagnostic completion/support need only.

Day 11 — Common Level II Start + Math Day 1

Show: one approved print/source containing a dimension, weld requirement, and material/source note. Have students point to each source location before calculating.

Whiteboard: SOURCE → CALCULATE → PLAN → VERIFY → RECORD.

Math: whole numbers + fraction sense using licensed Chasan Units 1–5 and original PCCC items.

Evidence: one source-cited calculation/verification record.

Day 12 — Blueprint I

Teach: object/hidden/center/dimension lines, multiple views, notes, units, datum/reference features, and what must not be assumed.

Ask: “Which view or note controls this answer?” Require students to point to it.

Math: fraction operations, Chasan Units 7–11.

Evidence: one independent print-reading decision with source location + units.

Day 13 — Blueprint II: Welding Symbols + Joint Details

Teach: joint type first, then arrow/reference line/side/tail from the authorized symbol source. Separate joint geometry from procedure conditions.

Rule: unclear or missing symbol information = STOP / CHECK.

Math: measurement and fraction/decimal equivalence, Chasan Units 6 & 16.

Evidence: joint/detail interpretation tied to the exact source feature.

Day 14 — Blueprint III: Sections, Assemblies + Sequence

Teach: why section/detail/assembly views exist and how one view can hide critical information.

Activity: trace overall assembly → part/detail → critical dimension → sequence constraint.

Record: five-step fabrication planning sequence. WLD-205 plans; WLD-210 performs.

Math: decimal operations + job-required rounding.

Day 15 — WPS/SWPS + Documentation Discipline

Teach source locating: process, filler/electrode, position, ranges/conditions, preheat/interpass where present. Never substitute memory for the approved procedure.

Documentation chain: actual setting/condition → measurement → inspection/check → corrective action → recheck.

Math: tolerance limits using Chasan Unit 17.

Evidence: completed source-locator/documentation record.

Day 16 — Trade Math I: Perimeter + Area

Start with meaning: perimeter is linear distance around; area is square coverage. Require correct unit type before formula use.

Model: one rectangular frame perimeter and one plate area from an approved print.

Evidence: independent print-based perimeter/area problem with units + reasonableness check.

Day 17 — Trade Math II: Triangles + Angles

Teach: identify knowns from the print before selecting the triangle/angle method. Tie calculations to gusset, bevel, or layout geometry.

Evidence: one independent geometry result plus how WLD-210 will physically verify it.

Math source: Chasan Units 27 and 35–37.

Day 18 — Trade Math III: Circles + Volume

Teach: diameter vs radius, circumference, circle area, cylinder/basic volume, and linear/square/cubic units.

Evidence: one round-work calculation with labeled units and reasonableness check.

Math source: Chasan Units 28–32.

Day 19 — Trade Math IV: Material Weight + Repeated Measurements

Teach: source value first. Students may not invent density/weight data. For repeated measurements, record originals before averaging.

Evidence: source value + calculation + unit + average/reasonableness check.

Math source: Chasan Units 20 & 34.

Day 20 — Trade Math V: U.S. Customary ↔ Metric

Teach: identify original unit, target unit, authorized conversion relationship, then convert. Keep original and converted values visible.

Rule: do not silently mix unit systems mid-calculation.

Evidence: one fabrication conversion with both units and source relationship.

Day 21 — Trade Math Readiness + Blueprint Integration

Diagnostic only: original PCCC readiness items, not secure AWS content.

Domains: measurement | fractions/decimals | geometry | tolerance | conversion | print-based operation selection.

Remediation: one weak domain, one parallel item, independent recheck.

Record: READY / CONTINUE SUPPORT by domain.

Day 22 — Official AWS SENSE II Trade Math Exam

Use only: authorized testing system/materials and approved procedure. Verify roster, accommodations, devices, security, and status-recording workflow.

LTG must never store: secure questions, answers, screenshots, copied prompts, or reconstructed exam content.

Record only: permitted completion/pass/retest/retrain status.

Day 23 — Metallurgy I: Carbon Steel

Teach: carbon steel as iron-carbon alloy; broad classes/source identification; what can and cannot be concluded from “carbon steel” alone.

Decision frame: MATERIAL → HEAT → EFFECT → RISK → CONTROL.

Evidence: one source-based carbon-steel decision. Exact grade/composition remains source-controlled.

Day 24 — Metallurgy II: Ferrite, Austenite, Pearlite + Transformation

Teach at working-welder depth: heating/cooling can change structure and properties in the HAZ; ferrite/austenite/pearlite are part of the transformation story.

Activity: trace one simple thermal-cycle scenario and separate source facts from assumptions.

Evidence: material → heat → structure/property → risk explanation.

Day 25 — Metallurgy III: Cooling Rate, TTT, Bainite, Martensite + HAZ

Teach: faster/slower cooling changes transformation risk direction; TTT is a decision aid, not permission to invent exact hardness or procedure settings.

Evidence: MATERIAL → HEAT → STRUCTURE/PROPERTY → RISK → CONTROL for one authorized scenario.

Day 26 — Metallurgy IV: Hardenability, Weldability + Cracking

Teach: hardenability is not hardness; weldability depends on material, joint, procedure, restraint and conditions; cracking observations do not prove one exact cause.

Evidence: one risk + governing source + verified prevention/control.

Day 27 — Metallurgy V: Stainless Steel

Teach: major stainless families, source-controlled grade identification, contamination control, heat/sensitization awareness, and why appearance is insufficient.

Evidence: one stainless-specific risk/control plus one unsupported inference the student refuses to make.

Day 28 — Metallurgy VI: Aluminum

Teach: alloy-series/heat-treatment awareness, oxide/cleanliness, high thermal conductivity, and possible HAZ property change. Procedure details come from the approved source.

Evidence: source → observable condition → risk → verified preparation/control.

Day 29 — Metallurgy VII: Residual Stress + Distortion

Teach: visible distortion vs residual stress, uneven heating/cooling, restraint, sequence, and source-controlled distortion-control planning.

Evidence: one risk + observation/measurement + permitted control/recheck.

Day 30 — Integrated Metallurgy Readiness

Diagnostic only: original PCCC non-secure readiness set covering carbon steel, stainless, aluminum, HAZ, stress/distortion, and prevention/control.

Remediation: weakest domain only, then one new parallel item.

Evidence: complete MATERIAL → HEAT → EFFECT → RISK → CONTROL decision with source.

Day 31 — Official AWS SENSE II Welding Metallurgy Exam

Use only: authorized secure exam system/materials and approved procedure.

No secure exam content in LTG. Do not display, duplicate, summarize, photograph, reconstruct, or coach secure items.

Record only: permitted status/evidence.

Day 32 — Blueprint/WPS/Governing-Source Integration

Source map: print → WPS/SWPS → material record → filler specification/certification → governing code/specification as applicable.

Teach: WPS/WPQR purpose, prequalified/SWPS concept, essential-variable/source-check habit at WLD-205 depth.

Evidence: one controlled change request stating what changed, why, and which governing source must be checked before action.

Day 33 — Common WLD-205 Capstone

Required chain: PRINT → MATH → METALLURGY → WPS/SOURCE → ACTUAL WLD-210 EVIDENCE → RECORD.

Before work: student identifies every source and what decision each controls.

Closeout: trace one technical decision end-to-end. Pending physical evidence must be labeled pending, never invented.

Evidence: complete traceable capstone packet and integrated job-math ticket.

Instructor boundary: protected course outcomes stay fixed. These directions improve delivery, source use, evidence, remediation, and instructor support only.
