# WLD 150 — Spring 6A provisional 24-day instructor guide

**Status:** Guide loaded into LTG as a draft. February 8–March 18, 2027 dates are provisional; no live section or attendance records until curriculum approval and section release.

**Role:** Advanced process specialization and weld performance. Paired daily with WLD 120.

**Class time:** 6:00–9:45 PM (225 minutes). Attendance follows the WLD 120 primary record.

## Daily teaching flow

Start with the safety gate and prior-day review. Demonstrate the day's method, supervise first attempts, release students for independent work only after setup approval, assess the listed evidence, then document corrections and close the shop. WLD 120 hands off approved parts and open issues to WLD 150 at 6:00 PM.

| Segment | Minutes | Clock time |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Toolbox talk and demonstration | 25 | 6:00 PM–6:25 PM |
| Guided setup and first attempt | 35 | 6:25 PM–7:00 PM |
| Welding practice, coaching, and assessment | 140 | 7:00 PM–9:20 PM |
| Evidence and safe closeout | 25 | 9:20 PM–9:45 PM |

## Day 1 — 2027-02-08 (candidate)

**Objective:** Refresh safety and assess starting skill

**Protected outcomes:** CLO 1

**Safety and instructor preparation:** PPE, ventilation, fire watch/hot-metal control, machine condition, and instructor release before operation. Stop work on unsafe setup or an unapproved procedure. Prepare the approved project drawings, WPS/test criteria, equipment, material, and individual evidence records.

**Opening and demonstration:** Review the prior checkpoint and today's release criteria. Demonstrate the method and quality check for: refresh safety and assess starting skill.

**Guided and independent work:** Inspect stations and run a supervised baseline coupon in the candidate primary process.

**Assessment/evidence:** Safety checklist and labeled baseline coupon

**Instructor sources:** 03-occupational-orientation.pdf

**Closeout:** Record evidence and open corrections; de-energize equipment, secure gas, control hot material, and clean the station.
