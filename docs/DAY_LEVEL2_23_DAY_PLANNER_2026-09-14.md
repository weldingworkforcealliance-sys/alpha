# PCCC Day Level II — 23-Day WLD 205 / WLD 210 Planner

Status: draft build specification. Do not activate for live instructors until QA is complete.

## Core synchronization rule

WLD 205 advances new theory only when PVHS Level II and PCCC Day Level II are both in attendance and can receive the same lesson from the same instructor at the same time. PVHS-only Fridays do not introduce new WLD 205 theory; they are shop/application/reinforcement days. If Day Level II meets while PVHS is absent, Day Level II also receives no new WLD 205 theory.

Day Level II WLD 210 is independently accelerated. Day Level II receives 300 minutes of WLD 210 shop time per day, while PVHS uses its longer calendar for the same protected WLD 210 skill/outcome progression.

## Guardrails

Protected course curriculum and course outcomes are not changed. The planner changes pacing, synchronization, implementation, instructor cues, resource placement, and evidence flow only. Secure AWS exam content must never be copied into LTG. Live Job Cards and LTG evidence records do not independently create AWS qualification or credential status.

## Activation gate

Before activation: verify all 23 WLD 205 days are 60 minutes; all 23 WLD 210 days are 300 minutes; each WLD 205 shared date matches the PVHS calendar; PVHS Friday hold days introduce no new WLD 205 theory; required resource cards and outcome links exist; Day Level II sections are not attached to live instructor views until final QA passes.
